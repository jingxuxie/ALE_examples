# Pre-tournament calibration

No fresh agent had been launched when these targets were fixed.

A 512-restart private search per case achieves 2.166397x overall, 5.686095x
on balanced bonds, 1.632634x on inhomogeneous bonds and 1.095245x on directional
bonds. Individual balanced-case gains range from 1.08x to 99.47x. The supplied
baseline uses 24 restarts. This establishes a real optimization gap, not a
requirement to reproduce an arbitrary target answer.

The final target is 4x overall and 1.1x in each family, with no case more than
5 percent worse. An initially considered 2x family threshold was relaxed before
any participant attempt because the directional family is much closer to the
baseline's apparent optimum. Its purpose is to prevent neglect of a family,
not to force the same absolute improvement on differently conditioned problems.

The private portfolio does not meet the final target; achievability is unknown.
