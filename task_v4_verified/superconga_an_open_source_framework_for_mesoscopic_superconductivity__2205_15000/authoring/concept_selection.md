# Discovery record

The source paper (arXiv:2205.15000, inspected full PDF) is a quasiclassical
framework, not a claim that an arbitrary reduced model is quantitatively
equivalent to Eilenberger theory. The selected CPU tasks explicitly identify
their approximations. No unavailable GPU prerequisite is used as hardness.

## Inspected seeds

- Paper sections IV–V: mesoscopic vortex shells, directed field sweeps,
  metastable branches, spectroscopy, geometry and pinning.
- Official SuperConga checkout, develop commit recorded in provenance.json.
- CHANGELOG: v1.1.0 metallic normal inclusions and impurity spectroscopy;
  real-energy self-energy and intermediate phase-shift corrections.
- Official research list: microscopic BdG comparison (2020), coreless
  vortices (2023), phase-crystal impurity diagram and patterned edge currents
  (2025). These are motivation, not undeclared extra participant requirements.
- Previous local pilot: synthetic continuation controller, 10-minute fresh
  attempt and 25-second evaluator timeout. Its failure is not accepted as
  evidence for the new one-hour experiments.

## Internal candidate pool

1. **A, selected:** low-energy vortex states in gauge-covariant GL grains.
   Nonconvex sector search survives after supplying exact energy/gradient.
2. **C, selected:** binary metallic-inclusion pattern realizing a prescribed
   multi-condition Andreev spectral fingerprint. Private physical design
   certifies feasibility; inverse design is the remaining problem.
3. **E, selected:** active impurity/vortex tomography from local BdG spectra.
   Combinatorial inverse scattering plus query selection, with a public model.
4. B: falsify a finite-angle Riccati quadrature error claim. Not selected:
   isolated grazing resonances may give an immediate standard counterexample.
5. F: repair impurity self-energies at intermediate phase shifts. Not selected:
   later upstream fix compresses to a small analytic transcription.
6. A: optimize a generic accelerator against arbitrary nonlinear maps.
   Rejected: previous pilot's maps have weak physical grounding and enumerated
   policy search is not a durable research bottleneck.
7. D: infer phase-crystal transition boundaries from precomputed data.
   Not selected: no sufficiently rich validated source-native dataset here;
   synthetic labels would risk turning into ordinary interpolation.
8. C: stable coreless chiral-vortex/skyrmion texture. Not selected: CPU reduced
   model parameters and stability certificate would require more independent
   physical validation than available, risking an impossible witness target.
9. A: accelerate Ozaki/Matsubara quadrature. Rejected: a standard rational
   approximation package would likely solve the compressed formulation.
10. C: robust pinning layouts for prescribed vortex arrays. Not selected:
   expensive relaxation-defined witnesses are harder to verify independently
   than direct Hermitian spectral fingerprints.

Only the three selected concepts are built. Each gets an independent ephemeral
ultima-alpha attempt with a 3600-second wall limit and participant-only
allowlisting. Targets are frozen before those sessions. Failed infrastructure
checks or timeouts without valid scoring are not evidence of scientific hardness.
