# Generation-only concept screening

Recorded 2026-08-28. Research sidecar only: not a tested participant, no fresh agents, no benchmark runs, and no measured hardness or performance claims. Ranking is an authoring recommendation, not an experimental result. Builder-owned implementations and other authoring files are outside this sidecar's scope.

**Recommendation:** prioritize A, C, E in that order. A has the richest compilation objective; C has the cleanest exact verifier; E adds active experimental design but requires identifiable anchors. Proposed evaluators use Python 3, NumPy/SciPy and optionally NetworkX, without Qiskit, actual hardware, OR-Tools or Z3.

## Ranked concepts

1. **A: Hardware-aware commuting phase-parity compilation — prioritize; baseline improvement.** Seed: phase-polynomial intermediate representations and target-aware synthesis in the paper, plus official GraySynth code [P1, S1, S2]. Jointly optimize weighted directed native CX operations, topology, shared parities and scheduling. Verify the final GF(2) map, phase-function equivalence modulo global phase, native edges and independently recomputed cost. Compare against routed GraySynth, weighted-tree synthesis and multistart parity-reuse baselines. The requested `cost <= 0.70 * best_portfolio_cost` is an **unvalidated target**, not a demonstrated attainable threshold. Reject plain GraySynth or independent-term compilation; audit phase conventions, residual permutations and baseline headroom.

2. **C: Compact graph-constrained linear reversible synthesis — prioritize; witness.** Seed: the paper's Boolean-linear representation and official PMH/all-to-all and KMS/line synthesis [P1, S1]. Publish GF(2) targets generated by concealed compact native circuits; request a native CX sequence under simultaneous strict gate/depth caps. Replay row operations exactly, check directed edges, and compute depth independently. The same private witness must satisfy both caps; it proves feasibility, not optimality or hardness. Arbitrary sparse graphs and tight joint caps distinguish this from one elimination algorithm. Reject generous all-to-all/line variants; guard against leaked generator seeds, free wire permutations and trivial cancellation-heavy targets.

3. **E: Adaptive cross-resonance calibration — prioritize conditionally; active design.** This is an **extension of the paper's calibration assumption**, not a calibration experiment demonstrated there: section IV.3 assumes calibrated partial-ZX gates and leaves calibration details out [P1]. Official CR characterization supplies a concrete six-term model and preparation/measurement protocol [S3]; it predates the paper. Adapt durations, preparations, measurement bases and shot allocation under noncommuting dynamics and finite-shot SPAM nuisance. Require **explicit trusted preparation/measurement anchors, a fixed frame/sign convention, and an identifiability audit** of the allowed nuisance family; zero-duration/reference probes must count toward the budget. Score held-out probability predictions or anchored process error, versus uniform and nonadaptive designs. Reject noiseless sinusoid fitting or unconstrained coefficient recovery with gauge ambiguities. A two-qubit matrix simulator is sufficient for the proposed evaluator.

4. **Robust dynamical-decoupling scheduling — reserve; baseline improvement / witness.** Seed: Appendix A's latency/crosstalk suppression, Appendix B's timing constraints, and official DD code [P2, S4]. Choose pulse axes/times under alignment, finite pulse duration, resource conflicts and uncertain noise. Verify ideal logical action and timing, then compare worst-case channel infidelity against no-DD, equispaced and XY4 baselines. Coupled noise and pulse errors provide the proposed difficulty. Reject perfect instantaneous echo against known static dephasing; do not score gate count as noise suppression.

5. **Multi-round dynamic parity-circuit repair — reserve; repair.** Seed: ancilla-mediated ZZ interactions, conditional correction/reset and the official follow-up tutorial [P2, S5]. Repair interacting faults in classical-bit mapping, correction placement, phase signs and reset across rounds with intervening rotations. Verify the full channel or specified measurement instrument, trace preservation and final ancilla reset; minimize edits/latency. Reject a single missing textbook correction. Basis-state-only tests miss phase errors; postselection is not deterministic repair; bound branch growth in the evaluator.

6. **Minimal diagnostic suites for unsafe rewrites — reserve; counterexample.** Seed: controlled-rotation commutation defects corrected by official PR #16337 [S6]. Generate compact preparation/circuit/measurement suites exposing a family of mutated rewrite rules across orientations and parameter regions. Independently verify observable discrepancies, mutation coverage and suite cost. Reject copying the published SWAP–CRZ–SWAP example or finding an eigenvector for one supplied matrix pair. Handle global phase and numerical tolerances correctly. The PR merged on 2026-06-08; this is a historical mutation seed, not a claim of a current defect.

7. **Fractional-gate legalization across pass boundaries — reserve; repair.** Seed: Runtime issue #2256's out-of-range RZZ gates after XXPlusYY synthesis [S7]. Supply a small pass/rewrite grammar and request a policy remaining legal after subsequent synthesis and optimization, with expansion limits. Check full-unitary equivalence and final target angle/edge constraints. Reject modulo-angle folding alone: later passes must be able to invalidate an earlier legalization. The issue is closed; closure alone is not evidence of a particular fix or present behavior.

8. **Budgeted selection of additional native-gate calibrations — reserve; active design / witness.** Seed: opportunistic partial-ZX support on selected target edges [P1]. Choose edge/angle capabilities under a calibration budget and submit compilation witnesses for a workload. Verify budget, semantics and aggregate weighted cost independently. Require complementary capabilities to change routing or parity reuse; independent per-edge gains reduce to ordinary knapsack. Distinct from A only when capability selection genuinely matters. Avoid scoring only the evaluator's preferred compiler.

9. **Dynamic-versus-SWAP crossover prediction — lower priority; prediction.** Seed: the paper's comparison and the follow-up tutorial's latency/DD treatment [P1, P2, S5]. Infer from limited noisy probes the relative performance at unseen depths/angles. Score held-out trajectory error and regret against choosing the better implementation. Require interacting coherent, measurement and latency effects. Reject known-parameter simulation, one exponential fit or a gate-count formula. Audit observability: sparse probes can make the requested extrapolation unidentifiable; test beyond trivial angles.

## Rejections and release gates

- Reject package reproduction, ordinary Gaussian elimination, standalone GraySynth, fixed noiseless Ramsey fitting, textbook echo insertion, copied bug reproducers and independent-edge knapsack as standalone tasks.
- No 30% improvement, cap tightness, runtime or participant difficulty has been validated here. The main agent must screen baseline headroom and concealed-witness feasibility before selecting thresholds.
- Freeze baseline implementations, seeds, trial counts and compute budgets. Official SABRE PR #14458 changed heuristic scaling [S8]; an unspecified current-package baseline is not reproducible.
- Require semantic verification independent of the submitted optimizer, explicit bit/phase/layout conventions, resource accounting and hidden generator separation. Numerical tolerances must not reward incorrect circuits.
- E cannot advance without identifiable trusted anchors. Prefer prediction of observable quantities over unidentifiable microscopic parameter scores.
