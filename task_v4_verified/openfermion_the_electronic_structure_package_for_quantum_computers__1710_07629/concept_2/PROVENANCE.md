## Why this is inverse topology synthesis

Four target occupied subspaces are generated by short, irregularly scheduled
native gates, then all circuit histories, schedules, angles, and seeds are withheld.
Participants receive only the occupied projectors, fixed initial occupations,
allowed hardware edges, and budgets. There are two shuffled ladder graphs and
two irregular cycle-with-chord graphs. Every planted circuit's used-edge support
is connected and spans all modes. Matchings vary by layer and instance; there is
no public common ansatz. No output circuit is compared to the planted topology:
any equivalent witness meeting the mathematical contract is accepted.

The budgets are substantially below a generic exact compilation, even before
counting routing. Solving the task requires exploiting the particular sparse
state preparation rather than merely diagonalizing the projector and reproducing
a conventional QR mesh. Both discrete edge/order/matching choices and continuous
complex rotation parameters matter. This is a design rationale, not a proof of
computational hardness; fresh isolated attempts are needed for empirical evidence.

The public baseline performs occupied-frame elimination, routes each nonlocal
rotation by signed swap conjugation, and schedules with per-mode dependencies.
Its large depth is measured, not substituted for a universal lower bound. For
additional context, the generic occupied-state mesh has k(n-k) slots: 49, 64,
64, and 81, also exceeding these gate budgets before graph routing. Those slot
counts are not measurements of an installed optimized compiler, and special
states can allow omitted gates. OpenFermion is absent from the actual environment.

## Numerical quality and independent verification

All targets are full-precision binary64 projectors produced by unitary conjugation,
not rounded low-precision energies. Their occupied/unoccupied eigenvalue gap is
1 to about 1e-15; the equivalent flat one-body Hamiltonian `I-2P` has gap 2.
Degeneracy within the occupied space is harmless because comparison is gauge
invariant. The 1e-8 Frobenius tolerance is absolute, not divided by mode count.
Both Frobenius error and Slater infidelity must pass; small energy error alone is
never scored. There is no nearly closing physical gap that could fake accuracy.

Generation rejects disconnected gate support, near-product modes, low-density
targets, adjacent per-wire repeated edges, and negligible gates. The smallest
single-gate deletion error is above 0.239 in every instance; target entries exceed
1e-9 on 88.9–100% of the matrix. These checks do not prove global circuit minimality.
Each planted circuit is 2 gates and 1 layer below its budgets, leaving modest
representation freedom without admitting a dense generic mesh.

The official evaluator composes full n-by-n layer matrices and forms the final
projector from the evolved occupied columns. The public simulator instead updates
only two orbital rows per gate. A third independent test constructs fermionic
creation/annihilation operators, including parity signs, in a fixed-particle Fock
sector and exponentiates their generators using SciPy. Determinant amplitudes,
covariances, many-body overlap, occupied-orbital gauge invariance, and signed-swap
routing agree to below 7e-16 in the recorded tests.

Official witness maximum projector error: `9.338771710542416e-16`.
Official baseline maximum projector error: `2.762036281030526e-15`.
Both are far below tolerance, so the baseline loses solely on real resources.

The independent verifier rejects duplicate JSON keys, extra fields, NaN/Infinity,
boolean/fractional indices, invalid angles, nonedges, colliding layers, incomplete
instance sets, excessive parser sizes, symbolic links, and FIFOs without blocking.
Only bounded regular JSON is read. Submitted code is never executed or imported.
Forty artifact checks pass in `adversary/validation_report.json`, including
projector-threshold boundary cases where fidelity alone would wrongly accept.

## Scores and freeze

- Main `core_score`: mean of the four strict certification indicators.
- `worst_family_score`: minimum of the two family means.
- `resource_score`: mean accuracy-gated `min(1, gate_budget/gates, depth_budget/depth)`,
  with denominator at least one. Secondary only; cannot rescue a failed certificate.
- `valid`: syntax/hardware/scheduling valid, independently of accuracy and budget.
- `passed`: all four instances certified. `runtime_score` is null; verifier time
  is measured but unscored. Participant development time is one hour.
