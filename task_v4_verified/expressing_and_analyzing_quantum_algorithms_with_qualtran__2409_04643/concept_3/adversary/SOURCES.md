# Private provenance

Seed: arXiv:2409.04643v1, Sections III.2–III.3 on coherent indexed operations
and quantum lookup data loading. Current official source inspected at commit
096a2d009059faee0cfae462c3d59cb055300eb9, including
qualtran/bloqs/data_loading/select_swap_qrom.py. The task is not a port of this
package or its block-size formula.

The six exact tables are generated by hidden layered/reconvergent and alternating
affine-feedback product networks. Their full truth tables, dimensions and budgets
are public. The generator, seeds and compact circuits are not mounted for tested
agents. No experimental data, optimality claim, cryptographic security claim or
hardware performance claim is made.

Every nonzero linear output combination has algebraic degree at least three;
the actual minima and all-input dependence checks are recorded in provenance.json.
The runnable shared-ANF baseline is exact on all rows but uses over eight times
the allowed AND budget in every instance. This is a checked synthesis gap rather
than a reference-answer comparison. The private circuit is one feasible design,
not a required canonical answer.

Validation uses three computational routes: row-by-row construction, packed-bit
exhaustive verification, and independently synthesized shared-ANF networks. The
private evaluator imports only its immutable local checker, never participant or
submission code. Negative tests cover wrong truth tables and invalid references.
