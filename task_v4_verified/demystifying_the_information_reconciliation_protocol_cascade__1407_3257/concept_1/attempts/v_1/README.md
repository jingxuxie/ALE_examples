# Submission

`policy.json` is the self-contained declarative submission. It requires no candidate Python code or external dependencies. The public simulator was not modified.

The policy bounds coarse first-pass partitions, uses transcript-derived parity estimates for the second pass, limits block growth when estimates are poorly supported, and varies quiet-pass stopping requirements with correction history. Small correction counts and empty transcripts receive longer confirmation schedules.

## Validation and limitation

`train_report.json` and `dev_report.json` are generated by the trusted public scoring implementation. `final_verification.log` records the final verification run. The training split cannot certify the Wilson confidence gate, even with zero observed failures.

Extensive additional experiments use locally generated operating tuples and independent seeds from the published distributions. Intermediate policies, frame records, and reports remain in this directory for reproducibility. Some intermediate policies satisfy the development target but fail additional reliability audits; those are not the final selection.

The final selection prioritizes reliability and expected cost across the public and independently generated cases. The required 3% bandwidth-family improvement is not consistently established. This submission therefore should not be treated as a fully validated solution to every improvement gate. No hidden evaluation was accessed or run.

Reports named `submission_summary.json`, `validation_summary.json`, `independent_summary.json`, and `audit_summary.json` describe an earlier candidate; their embedded policy hashes distinguish them from the final artifact. Use `dev_report.json`, `train_report.json`, and `final_verification.log` for the final policy.
