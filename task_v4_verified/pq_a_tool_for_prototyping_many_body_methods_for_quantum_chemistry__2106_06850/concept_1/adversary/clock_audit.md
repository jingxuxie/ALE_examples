The first sandbox baseline evaluation charged bubblewrap initialization to the planner's 30-second clock. On this shared host, sandbox setup sometimes exceeded the planner budget even though the exact baseline is computationally inexpensive. Its result is retained as `baseline_setup_clock_incident.json`, excluded from hardness evidence. The corrected evaluator starts a trusted executor inside the initialized sandbox; only the planner subprocess is given the 30-second solving budget. Setup wall time and actual planner wall time are separately recorded. The mathematical instances, score thresholds and participant interface were unchanged. This is an evaluator-infrastructure repair, not a challenge ratchet.
