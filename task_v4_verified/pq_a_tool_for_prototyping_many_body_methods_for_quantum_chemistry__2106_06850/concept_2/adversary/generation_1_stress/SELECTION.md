# Champion–challenger ratchet 1

Both independent fresh sessions solved generation 1. The stronger champion even has an exact right ground eigenvector to numerical precision, so merely tightening endpoint energy accuracy would not create a meaningful challenge.

Their density matrices, however, have substantial non-Hermitian components. This is an obvious, basis-independent density-quality signal rather than an arbitrary hidden test. The private sweep tests Hamiltonian neighborhoods, orthogonal orbital bases, and complex orbital probes and clusters endpoint failures. Its reproducible data are in `report.json`.

Generation 2 focuses on a **Hermiticity-quiet population failure**: add `||gamma-gamma.T||_F/sqrt(3) <= 0.001`, retaining the original population threshold and every physical/root/accuracy constraint. This scale is motivated by the density-asymmetry diagnostic, not by a hidden model identifier. The task challenges a supplied stronger screening heuristic; neither the 2021 seed paper nor the following work asserts the universal implication being falsified.
