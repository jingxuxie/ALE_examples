# Private quartic-scaling controls

Two additional 512-site, lambda4=0.02 controls complement the 256/512-site
lambda4=0.005 probe. They use the same explicitly private 64 MiB file-cap fork,
unchanged finite Hamiltonian and CPU/wall guards, and 600-CPU-second variational
teachers. They are outside the current public domain, are not an official grade,
and do not constitute a new task generation. Source hashes and actual states are
retained; no exact ground energy or budget-feasible full solver is asserted.
