# Private residual variational search, tranche 1

Only this directory may be modified. No task generation, target/domain change,
fresh-agent launch, frozen evaluation, or package/timing failure claim is made.
Completed v3 and v4 sources are byte-for-byte private snapshots. Production v3
has no `mps.py`; its tensor routines live in `optimizer.py`. The corrected prior
teacher and the frozen trusted contractor are separately snapshotted.

Initial allocation: four uniform parity/mass controls, then six in-domain
spatially disordered/weak-link/multi-region probes. At most twelve initial
requests and approximately thirty minutes of numerical work in this tranche.
Larger/higher-bond cases require an explicit label and are not selected initially.
Masses are coordinates to explore finite-basis responses, not exact critical
points. The finite padded-oscillator projected Hamiltonian is the objective.

All energy statements come from the unchanged trusted contractor applied to
retained finite, bond-capped states in the original request's parity sector.
Reference energies are attainable variational energies, not exact energies.
The scientific screen is E40 - Eattainable > 1e-7 * n_sites. Timing observations
are preserved separately and do not establish a scientific failure.

Direct native-child wait4 measurements include imports and serialization; they
are private diagnostics, not frozen runner certification. BLAS/OMP are one;
children have a 2 GiB address-space limit. Both solver and teacher outputs are
loaded with the trusted 8 MiB archive and tensor validator. No cap enlargement
or convention change is used to create a purported failure.
