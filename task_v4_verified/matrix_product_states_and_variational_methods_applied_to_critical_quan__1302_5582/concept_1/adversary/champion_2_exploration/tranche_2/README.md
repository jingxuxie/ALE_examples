Authorized after tranche 1's genuine in-domain gap. Twelve distinct initial
Hamiltonian/sector/cap requests; at most 1,200 additional CPU seconds of numerical
work. All start with 40-second cold v4 and v3 comparisons. Reference refinements
and replications use only the remaining compute allowance.

Every chain remains N64/d14 within the advertised coefficient domain. Six odd,
four even, and two nonzero-field unrestricted problems span caps 12/14/16/20.

parity, and field competition. The fixed absolute screen is 6.4e-6. Crossover
coordinates are motivated by tranche 1's attained parity response, not asserted
finite-basis critical masses.

selection rationale. `SUMMARY.json` distinguishes achieved same-cap references
from exact energies. A collection of screen-positive probes is not an admitted
suite or a proof of full frozen-target feasibility. No public/evaluator change,
new formal generation, fresh coding agent, or domain extension is authorized here.
