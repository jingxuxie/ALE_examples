# Empirical decision

## Concept 01: rejected, not redesigned

The valid isolated `ultima-alpha` session completed in 1749.118 seconds, without
timing out, and left the participant source unchanged. Its scientific core
score is **1.000**, with **1.000 in every one of the six hidden families**.
The preserved transcript shows a real baseline/diagnosis/repair/refinement loop,
not access to private labels. The model also created independent identities,
additional near-threshold and hierarchy stress studies, and a portable handoff.

The shortcut risk identified during source triage materialized. The model
replaced the inherited migration with a conventional hybrid: analytic Laurent
and local Taylor processing of the parameter representation, causal simplex
deformation with quadrature/adaptive subdivision, Dirichlet gamma expansions
for the supported infrared triangles, and known continued-dilogarithm formulas
for zero/one-mass boxes. This is sophisticated scientific programming, but the
bounded task does not empirically exceed frontier capability. The universal
parameter-moment representation also shares too much structure across the
finite, UV, and local-matching parts of the campaign.

The concept is rejected under the >=0.90 rule. No additional hidden cases,
stricter scientific thresholds, expanded formats, or harder parameter choices
were introduced after the result. No fundamental redesign occurred. Missing
clerical artifacts or an artificial time limit are not being used as hardness.

## Grader compatibility audit

The initial full grade already had core 1.000 and overall 0.9644. Its evidence
parser unnecessarily restricted claim table filenames and assumed every figure
used production as its comparison profile. The submission validly included
separate inherited-baseline runners, direct-profile comparisons, and additional
stress tables. The parser was broadened to rerun declared configurations and
submitted request files, recompute the relevant table values, and independently
check those comparisons. The initial grade is preserved as
`evaluation_initial_schema.json`; the final grade is `evaluation.json`.

This repair changes neither participant files, hidden cases, numerical targets,
scientific scoring, nor the hardness outcome. A new frontier session is not
needed to establish success: the original completed session already exceeded
the rejection boundary, and no participant failure was inferred from the parser
limitation. The final evaluator also rechecks the known-good reference.

## Remaining source-derived concepts

After this rejection, the four other source-triage concepts were reconsidered
as genuinely different workflows, not mutations of Concept 01:

- Light-by-light reproduction remains a complete-author-notebook or known-
  helicity-formula shortcut. Energy/angle limits alone do not supply five
  independent hidden families; stripping the author solution creates a timed
  reimplementation rather than a defensible research workflow.
- CAS migration remains dominated by unsupported proprietary interpreter
  primitives rather than the central scientific task. A compatibility failure
  cannot be counted as frontier scientific difficulty.
- Singular-threshold matching is outside the regular-Taylor capability claimed
  by the paper. The narrow version becomes a standard fit; the broad version
  has no verified known-good general solution in the available environment.
- Scalar-D0 continuation alone is directly available from standard loop
  libraries, or collapses to the same numerical/analytic hybrid that the fresh
  model has now successfully constructed.

No remaining concept passes the prerequisite gates. They are rejected before
construction rather than manufacturing a knowingly weak second pilot to fill
a quota. Exactly one concept was built, within the maximum of three. There is
no retained task. Final paper status: `rejected`, reason
`paper_did_not_yield_frontier_hard_task`.
