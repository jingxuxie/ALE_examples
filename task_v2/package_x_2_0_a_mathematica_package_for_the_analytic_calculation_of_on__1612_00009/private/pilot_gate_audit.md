## Scientific connection and artifacts

The bounded outcome exercises weighted four-point coefficients, metric and
dimensional shifts, UV/IR-origin accounting, causal real-kinematics evaluation,
and mixed Taylor coefficients. These are central supported operations of the
paper, rather than an unrelated inference problem. The experimental claim
being audited is consistent and numerically stable evaluation of the coupled
workflow, including exceptional Gram geometries and widely separated overall
scales. This task does not claim to reproduce every feature of the package or
to establish arbitrary-precision evaluation from a double-precision method.

The paper is an analytic software paper, not a dataset paper. After inspecting

## Ownership and decisions

Meaningful components are parameter/routing preparation, weighted tensor and
dimensional coefficient computation, numerical continuation, local matching,
subtraction/observable integration, experiment orchestration, convergence and
resource evaluation, visualization from measurements, and technical reporting.

Consequential choices not prescribed by TASK.md include:

1. When a real-domain integration, contour/boundary representation, or local
   expansion is justified. Fixed-width and causal boundary answers can agree
   in a spacelike check but disagree substantially in the physical cut.
2. Whether epsilon sampling at fixed quadrature, exact Laurent processing, or
   endpoint subtraction is defensible. UV traces and massless vertices provide
   different evidence; naive extrapolation misses endpoint pole origins.
3. Whether numerical differences or differentiation of the regularized object
   can support mixed matching coefficients at exceptional geometry and under
   scale changes. Moderate-order scalar agreement does not settle this choice.
4. Whether one fixed work level or an adaptive/regime-specific policy is worth
   its cost. The public profiles and refinement disagreements are informative
   but do not give the hidden correct answers.

The workflow explicitly requires measured inherited outputs, diagnosis,

## Hidden heterogeneity and shortcut audit

There are six pilot cases, not six seeds of one random generator:

- Weighted, off-shell, positive-mass Euclidean four-point tensors: raised
  propagator weights and moment normalization, with no cuts or poles.
- On-shell external scattering through a massive box above normal threshold:
  causal complex continuation, a different external geometry and analytic region.
- UV-divergent tensor self energy plus a tadpole pinch and a dimension-eight
  four-point coefficient: Laurent normalization and epsilon-dependent traces.
- Soft/collinear massless three-point functions: endpoint poles of different
  orders disappear under different tensor insertions; invariant derivatives.
- Massless zero- and one-off-shell-leg box functions: overlapping channels,
  dilogarithmic continuation, and double infrared poles.
- Rank-deficient Gram geometries used for mixed mass/invariant matching:
  nonsingular local jets and scale-covariant weighted/metric representations.

Changing an overall scale does NOT count as another family. Neither do different
weights, moments, or random seeds alone. The six families differ in topology,
pole origin/endpoint structure, causal region, and geometry/operation.

One real quadrature call does not handle all of them. One fixed imaginary
regulator cannot recover the Laurent coefficients. One scalar library call
does not produce weighted moments, mixed local coefficients, and dimensional
counterterm products. Nonetheless, a compact hybrid of known analytic tools
and numerical integration is a serious shortcut risk. If the fresh model
successfully constructs that hybrid, the concept must be rejected rather than
made larger or assigned harsher thresholds. A failed attempt is also insufficient
if the ultimate task is judged to violate the standard-shortcut gate.

The scientific score is 0.8 times the mean family score plus 0.2 times the
minimum family score. Individual scores measure relative digits continuously
up to nine, so near-reference accuracy is rewarded. Missing clerical artifacts
do not lower that core score. Overall scoring additionally includes measured
runtime and checked evidence, with a resident-memory overrun penalty. Efficiency
uses measured child CPU time, normalized to the strong reference's 5.13 CPU
seconds; wall time is also recorded and remains the execution ceiling. This
avoids scoring unrelated contention on the shared host as model performance.

The evaluator reruns the submitted service in a separate mount/network namespace
with only system runtime, the read-only submission, a request file, and scratch
space. Private source and gold values are not mounted. It reruns the public
production and ablation profiles, checks coefficients, work, configuration hashes,
table-backed claims, and figure source data. Reporting is manually audited as
well. Resource accounting occurs inside the execution namespace.
