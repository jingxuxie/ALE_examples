# Final decision: reject, not a frontier-hard candidate

The isolated **ultima-alpha** participant completed normally in 1861.85 seconds,
about 31 minutes of its one-hour allowance. Its full-channel and quadratic-response
core score is effectively 1.0 across all six held-out regimes. The public input
tree is byte-for-byte unchanged. No hidden targets or author solution were exposed.
The reference independently passed all hidden cases, the public replay audit,
17 tests, finite-amplitude response cross-checks, and a convergent comparison with
the official full second-order filter calculation.

## What made the pilot too easy

The agent independently found the same standard solution structure as the strong
reference: auxiliary moment propagation for the complete quadratic response;
stationary Gaussian quadrature; a Hermite stochastic-Liouville treatment of OU
noise; finite-state propagation for switching noise; and the white-noise generator.
All supplied colored covariances have convenient finite Markov embeddings. The
many-component weak regime needs only a checked quadratic forecast. Those known
methods generalize across the hidden devices without genuinely open-ended research.

The agent did not merely match a scalar fidelity, exploit a public target matrix,
or omit the requested investigation. It found the one-sided-spectrum problem,
static regularization loss, bath-restart error, missing coherent terms, and the
finite-noise failure of Gaussian operator-cumulant closure. It ran amplitude,
noise-law, memory, refinement, representation and scaling experiments and produced
the requested executable system, tables, figures and report. The remaining raw
evidence-audit deductions are not substantive scientific failures. In particular,
the audit's handling of supplementary evidence tables and rich figure-source
metadata is limited; these deductions cannot establish hardness.

The core threshold for rejection was fixed before the run. No tighter tolerances,
extra dimensions, new hidden cases, or more elaborate output requirements were
introduced after success. There was no fundamental redesign.

## Remaining concept gate review

The five initial concepts and their gate decisions are retained in `candidates.md`.
After the pilot succeeded, the alternatives were reconsidered rather than simply
making the same predictor harder by adding a new spectrum or geometry:

- Conditional measurement/feedback histories require a workflow outside the
  paper's supported endpoint-process result. Small bounded versions additionally
  admit standard augmented-state forward propagation. They fail the centrality
  and shortcut gates as a replacement task.
- The actual second-order limiting-case regression is an ordinary numerical
  repair, not a new professional research workflow.
- The RB reproduction is already substantially implemented in the real example;
  remaining fitting and uncertainty work is routine.
- QFT assembly/performance is already handled by visible concatenation and
  extension patterns. Making it larger would be a scale mutation, not a genuinely
  different qualifying concept.

Consequently **one pilot was built and screened, not two**. No second construction
was forced after all remaining ideas failed pre-build gates. This is a bounded
negative result for this search, not a claim that the paper could never motivate
a harder research project. No task is retained or labeled frontier-hard.
