# Pre-screen construction audit

The author-code extracts and CPU replacement retain the real coupled pipeline:
prepared complex condensate -> local phase intervention -> rotating nonlinear
evolution -> signed subgrid cores -> material-aware triangulation -> spatial bond
correlations and kinetic decomposition -> controlled comparison -> scientific
conclusion. Nine substantive components are present without counting output
formatting: preparation interpretation, physical implementation, configuration,
simulation, numerical stability, orchestration, evaluation, visualization and
evidence-supported reporting.

Consequential choices are not a requirement to reproduce the reference method:
1. An explicit adaptive Hamiltonian method, symmetric directional splitting, and
   rotation-via-shears can all be scientifically valid; step selection must be
   based on refinement/conservation and cost, not on the baseline's normalization.
2. Local zero estimation can use affine least-squares, complex bilinear roots, or
   higher-order interpolation. The evidence distinguishes signed phase cores
   from density minima and shows how noisy/vacuum regions affect estimates.
3. A measured discrepancy may originate in propagation, signed-core detection,
   finite-domain neighborhood construction, or current partition. Which repairs
   are sufficient must be established by rerunning controls and interventions;
   unit norm alone is deliberately not a full oracle.
4. The participant chooses the ablation and convergence experiments, and must
   distinguish a real finite-system failure to preserve high order from a
   measurement convention's bias. Reporting a relative intervention hierarchy
   is not evidence of almost-perfect background order or indefinite stability.

The contract defines observables precisely so convention mismatch is not used as
hardness. It necessarily describes what is measured, but it does not supply a
propagator, a correct estimator, parameter tuning, target main outputs, labels,
or the scientific result. The several plausible approaches need not share the
reference's implementation. A five-family acceptance campaign exercises geometry
and domain connectivity, not five random draws of one coordinate generator.

The known-good solution passes the small public calibration, has second-order
convergence against independent DOP853 Fourier-Hamiltonian evolution, and scores
0.999585 core against dt=0.0005 targets at dt=0.002. The baseline's failures are
scientific, not execution failures. Runtime is tens of seconds per full campaign,
so a one-hour participant budget is not an artificial simulation bottleneck.

Important residual shortcut risk: the complete repair may still be achievable
by combining ordinary splitting, plaquette winding, Delaunay graphs and Helmholtz
projection. This is why the isolated empirical screen is dispositive. No claim
of frontier hardness is made before that screen. A high score means rejection,
not permission to add stricter edge cases or change the scoring thresholds.

The paper PDF/text, full upstream checkout, generation code, completed solution,
targets, evaluator, preceding attempts, and grading logs are outside the two
paths mounted by the mandated launcher. The launched session is ephemeral,
approval=never, model=ultima-alpha, effort=high; only v_01 participant and attempt
paths are passed. Source-data provenance is explicit; no reconstruction is
misrepresented as an author's deposited measurement.
