# Pre-screen gates and limits

This is a pilot, not a claim that one failure establishes a universal frontier boundary.

- Contribution: lead-resolved many-body scattering-state expectation values, stationary-background time propagation, numerical treatment of infinite leads. The pulse/cavity and gated honeycomb observables exercise the paper's scientific demonstration. Bound-sector completion is part of its formal initial-state decomposition, not a loosely related inference problem.
- Artifact tier: realistic benchmark-authored portability workspace retaining the official model/occupation/band-analysis/quadrature/initialization/boundary/evolution/observable/experiment separation. Official example files and integration utilities are authentic, unmodified artifacts. Injected release-candidate shortcomings are explicitly benchmark-authored and are not attributed to an upstream commit. Source archive and paper are private.
- Ownership: model interpretation, initial-state construction, numerical integration, open-boundary stability, time simulation, configuration, experiment orchestration, convergence/ablation, performance measurement, figure source data and scientific interpretation. Formatting does not count as a component.
- Decisions: (1) equilibrium finite filling vs lead-resolved stationary preparation, distinguished by unequal-contact control; (2) evolving an occupied sea vs propagating deviations or an alternative boundary representation, distinguished by no-drive drift and boundary scans; (3) apparent continuum convergence vs spectral completeness, distinguished by localized-sector control; (4) refine time, spectrum or lead representation based on separate experimental evidence. Public TASK provides the physical definition, not which numerical strategy is the reference.
- Loop: the baseline is executable and passes microscopic convention tests but drifts in no-drive control. A high-level smoothness/convergence check cannot certify an independently occupied reservoir or an omitted localized sector.
- No oracle: no target public traces, main-data labels, hidden values, paper, reference implementation or evaluator is in participant access. Tiny tests establish current orientation, retarded branch and phase convention only.
- Families: voltage-driven scalar cavity; localized/dark-state release; flux ring with three unequal reservoirs; spin-mixing noncommuting drive; honeycomb multichannel bath; gapped, rank-deficient dimerized leads. Geometry/model/reservoir structure changes, not six seeds.
- Shortcut audit: uniform finite Fermi filling cannot implement the specified nonequilibrium state; a stationary transmission formula cannot give transients; propagating a handful of Fermi-level waves misses the occupied continuum; continuum-only quadrature misses discrete spectral weight; a flat-band contact discards band thresholds and memory. A generic solver that correctly combines all required stages is an accepted solution, not prohibited for being general.
- Evaluation: six hidden cases, mean plus worst-family fidelity, independent runtime/RSS, and evidence reruns. Accuracy is continuous, with initial-state and transient components separated. A density offset cannot drown out current error. Resource and evidence scores do not determine the core hardness classification.
- Reference: full finite-system equilibrium spectral checks and independent DOP853 transient checks precede hidden convergence comparison. A shallow-bound-state omission was discovered and repaired in preflight; it is not counted as a participant attempt or a redesign. A multichannel preflight geometry was bounded to keep the pilot evaluator short; the physical workflow and six families are unchanged. All these changes occur before any participant screening.
- Isolation: exact requested allowlist launcher, exact requested ultima-alpha model, ephemeral new session, 3600-second wall limit. Only current participant version and the attempt's empty output are supplied; transcript and grader are outside the allowlist. Evaluation executables run in a separate no-network bubblewrap namespace without hidden answers or sibling workspaces.
- Failure audit commitment: a timeout, incomplete plotting, dependency problem, or nearly correct implementation is not called hard. The final status depends on the numerical failure modes and measured core score, not this gate checklist.
