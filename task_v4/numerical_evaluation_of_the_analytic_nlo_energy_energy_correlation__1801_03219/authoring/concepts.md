# Concept selection (frozen before tournament)

The seed is arXiv:1801.03219, whose actual preprint title is *The Energy-Energy
Correlation at Next-to-Leading Order in QCD, Analytically*. The input task name
describes numerical use of that result, not the bibliographic title.

Considered concepts:

1. Direct evaluation of the published eleven-function formula: rejected as
   formula transcription, even though endpoint cancellation is severe.
2. Integer reconstruction of rational prefactors from rounded calibration:
   prior local attempt failed, but finite-precision identifiability is unclear;
   not retained as a new concept.
3. **A: joint rate-distortion compression of color-resolved EEC densities,
   angular derivatives and finite-bin moments.** Selected. A shared partition
   and fixed scalar budget force a nonlinear approximation allocation problem;
   a dense table or the native formula is not an admissible answer.
4. **B: reproduce false numerical confidence in a frozen weighted-EEC
   integrator.** Selected. Requires a constrained, material, independently
   reproduced integration failure, not disagreement with a second weak rule.
5. **C: construct a momentum-balanced sparse energy-flow event with a prescribed
   complete discrete EEC.** Selected. Exact autocorrelation constraints make
   this a discrete inverse problem rather than forward formula evaluation.
6. E: query-efficient color-factor experimental design: rejected because the
   basic linear version collapses to standard Fisher-information design.
7. F: repair a multi-file event-analysis pipeline: not selected; no authentic
   upstream behavioral defect has yet justified a repair benchmark.
8. D: extract a coupling from real EEC data: not selected because a credible
   held-out ground truth and uncertainty model are not available locally.
9. B: construct any homometric pair of circular events: rejected because an
   unconstrained convolution-factor construction can make this too easy.
10. A: optimize source-native NLO subtraction sampling: promising, but a faithful
    expensive four-parton simulator would require substantially more validation
    than the three selected concepts.

Only concepts 3, 4 and 5 are built. Source material, native calculations,
prior attempts, hidden cases and planted witnesses stay outside participant/.
All targets are fixed before their corresponding fresh-agent launch. A failed
attempt is not evidence of impossibility. A private witness is only counted as
achievability when it passes the same public constraints and private evaluator.
