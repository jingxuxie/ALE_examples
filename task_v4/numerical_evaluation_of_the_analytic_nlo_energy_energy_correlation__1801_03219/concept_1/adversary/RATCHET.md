# Champion-to-endpoint ratchet

Generation one is solved: the fresh agent's 268-scalar density artifact passes
the original evaluator, a 60,001-point broad sweep, finite bins, signed color
combinations and one-sided knots. Its maximum tolerance ratio is 0.00124203.
No original-contract counterexample was found. The immutable original task and
grader are in `champions/generation_1/task_snapshot/`.

The private downstream search then applies the paper's exact leading-power
subtractions to extract the next-to-leading-power endpoint information. This is
an explicit strengthened scientific objective, not a retrospective assertion
that generation one failed its original contract. Exact coefficient replay and
160/220-dps native evaluations reveal two failure groups: collinear residual
and slope loss, and backward residual and slope loss. The collinear slope can
even be wrong by more than its own magnitude while the full density passes.
The independent derivation and deep 200/280-dps checks are in `nlp_search/`.

Generation two permits structural residual charts, so the objective is not an
unavoidable subtraction-roundoff trap. It grades residual values and slopes
directly, while reconstructing the physical density for finite bins. The
storage budget is the actual champion's 268-scalar footprint. The old champion
is the supplied runnable baseline. Mixed tolerances are unchanged; the new
fixed target requires every family and a minimum 100-fold reduction of the
baseline maximum ratio. The new baseline ratio is 9,273,303.975896852.

The new oracle uses 140-dps native values, 180-dps complex-step derivatives,
independent degree-24 and degree-40 panels, and an independent native derivative
crosscheck of the deployment arithmetic. The largest refinement discrepancy is
about 2.1e-12, well below the target tolerances. No known 268-scalar passing
generation-two artifact is presumed. It will remain open if the fresh agent
fails and no same-budget construction establishes achievability.
