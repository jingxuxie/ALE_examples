# Differentiable compact-field transport

Complete `workspace/solve.py` for the density-carrying gauge-field transport
defined in `input/contract.md`. Preserve the physical group and return
accurate transport, density, and sensitivities at the specified lattice sizes.

Submit `solve.py` and any helpers in your output directory. The evaluator
invokes `input/runtime/bin/python3.12 solve.py INPUT.npz OUTPUT.npz`.
The starter is an identity baseline, not a working transport. No network
access or additional downloads are needed.
